**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft for legal review and publication  
**Applies to:** Buyers, Licensees, Sellers/Publishers, Technology Owners, Service Providers, Partners, and other Marketplace Participants

## 1. Purpose and Scope
TechFoundX is a technology marketplace and discovery/transaction facilitation platform. It may provide catalog, listing, verification, communications, due-diligence, partner discovery, licensing, offer, and transaction-support features.

These Terms govern use of TechFoundX websites, applications, marketplace interfaces, APIs, communications, listings, and related services. Product-specific or transaction-specific agreements may supplement these Terms; an expressly applicable signed agreement controls for its subject matter.

## 2. Marketplace Role
Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, TechFoundX is not the seller, licensor, developer, employer, agent, owner, guarantor, or manufacturer of third-party offerings.

Verification, publication, badges, ranking, or marketplace availability do not constitute an endorsement, warranty, certification, guarantee of ownership, performance, security, solvency, suitability, or future results.

## 3. Participant Categories
TechFoundX may support:
- Buyers acquiring technology or services.
- Licensees obtaining rights to use technology.
- Sellers and publishers listing technology or services.
- Technology owners and IP rights holders.
- Professional and technical service providers.
- Strategic, commercial, implementation, investment, or integration partners.
- Reviewers and other authorized participants.

## 4. Eligibility and Accounts
Participants must provide accurate, current, and complete information. TechFoundX may require identity, business, ownership, payment, tax, authority, funds, or other verification before particular features are available.

Participants are responsible for account activity and must protect credentials, authentication factors, API keys, and confidential access information.

## 5. Identity and Business Verification
TechFoundX may use appropriate lawful evidence such as government ID, corporate records, tax/business information, beneficial-ownership information, domain/email verification, payment information, and proof of authority.

Verification confirms that information met the applicable process at the relevant time; it is not a guarantee of conduct, solvency, ownership, quality, or future performance. Forged, altered, stolen, misleading, or unauthorized evidence is prohibited.

## 6. Seller and Publisher Obligations
A Seller must:
1. Have the legal right and authority to list and offer the technology/service.
2. Provide accurate identity, ownership, licensing, pricing, availability, compatibility, limitations, and support information.
3. Disclose material dependencies, third-party components, open-source obligations, restrictions, and limitations.
4. Maintain reasonable support and security processes appropriate to the offer.
5. Respond truthfully to verification and due-diligence requests.
6. Keep listings current and correct material inaccuracies promptly.
7. Not list technology it is not authorized to sell, license, distribute, or represent.
8. Honor accepted contracts, offers, licenses, and disclosed commercial commitments.

## 7. Listing Accuracy
Listings must be clear, current, and materially accurate. They must not fabricate customers, revenue, adoption, certifications, security claims, performance metrics, ownership, IP rights, or capabilities.

AI, automation, third-party APIs, models, agents, data pipelines, and material technical dependencies must be described accurately where relevant. TechFoundX may request evidence for material claims.

## 8. Buyer and Licensee Obligations
Buyers and Licensees must provide accurate information, conduct appropriate legal/technical/financial/operational/security/commercial due diligence, review terms before commitment, respect granted rights, protect confidential information, and avoid unlawful, fraudulent, deceptive, abusive, or impersonating conduct.

Marketplace verification does not replace independent due diligence.

## 9. Pricing and Commercial Information
Where a listing type requires an asking price or commercial position, the Seller must provide a genuine and supportable position.

**No Price / No Commercial Position — No Transaction Listing:** a transaction listing may remain unpublished until required commercial information is supplied.

Applicable taxes, marketplace fees, payment-processing charges, and other amounts must be disclosed where applicable.

## 10. Licensing
A license is not a copyright/IP ownership transfer unless the executed agreement expressly says so.

License terms should define, as applicable: parties, technology/version, permitted use, territory, field of use, duration, renewal, exclusivity, sublicensing, fees, payment schedule, support, maintenance, updates, security, restrictions, termination, post-termination rights, data, and confidentiality.

## 11. Intellectual Property
Unless expressly transferred by valid written agreement, each party retains its IP rights. Listing technology does not itself transfer copyright, patents, trademarks, trade secrets, source-code ownership, or other IP.

A Seller represents that it has the rights and permissions necessary for its listing and proposed transaction.

## 12. Third-Party and Open-Source Components
Sellers must disclose material third-party and open-source components when they affect licensing, redistribution, security, support, ownership, or customer obligations. Third-party technology must not be represented as the Seller's own where that would be misleading or unlawful.

## 13. Confidential Information and Private Listings
TechFoundX may support confidential listings and controlled due diligence.

A confidential workflow may require:
**Buyer interest → Qualification → Verification → NDA → Seller approval → Controlled disclosure → Access logging.**

Confidential information may only be used for the authorized purpose.

## 14. Communications
TechFoundX may provide text, email, chat, audio, video, notifications, or other communication features.

Harassment, threats, impersonation, fraud, malware delivery, credential theft, unauthorized access, spam, unlawful solicitation, and control circumvention are prohibited.

Where lawful and disclosed, relevant communications may be retained/reviewed for security, fraud prevention, disputes, compliance, and marketplace integrity.

## 15. Offers, Negotiations, and Deals
An inquiry, message, expression of interest, listing, or preliminary offer does not create a binding transaction unless the required acceptance and contracting process is completed.

Participants must review final commercial and legal terms before acceptance.

## 16. Transactions and Payments
Where TechFoundX provides payment functionality, payment may be processed by TechFoundX or an authorized provider under applicable payment terms.

Where parties transact directly, they remain responsible for their payment, tax, delivery, support, and contractual obligations unless TechFoundX expressly agrees otherwise.

Transactions may be delayed/restricted for fraud, sanctions/compliance, identity, payment-risk, legal, or security reasons.

## 17. Fees
TechFoundX may charge disclosed listing, success, transaction, subscription, verification, or service fees. No undisclosed marketplace fee should be represented as payable. Taxes may apply.

## 18. Professional and Technical Services
Service providers must accurately describe qualifications, scope, deliverables, pricing, timelines, dependencies, and limitations. Regulated services require applicable professional authorization.

## 19. Reviews, Ratings, and Marketplace Content
Reviews must reflect genuine experience. Fake, purchased, manipulated, retaliatory, or unauthorized reviews are prohibited. Ranking manipulation and misleading testimonials are prohibited.

TechFoundX may moderate, restrict, remove, or request evidence for violating content.

## 20. Prohibited Conduct
Prohibited conduct includes fraud, impersonation, unauthorized technology, IP misrepresentation/infringement, fake reviews, shill activity, malware/ransomware, credential theft, unauthorized access, bypassing controls, harassment/threats, money laundering/sanctions evasion, fee circumvention, confidential-data abuse, prohibited scraping, and misleading security/AI/compliance/certification/revenue/customer/performance claims.

## 21. Off-Platform Circumvention
Participants must not use TechFoundX to identify counterparties and intentionally bypass disclosed marketplace fees, verification requirements, transaction safeguards, or applicable marketplace controls.

Enforcement may include warning, formal notice, communication restriction, listing/transaction restriction, account review, suspension, or termination. Severe fraud or security abuse may result in immediate action.

## 22. Security
Participants must not compromise TechFoundX systems, accounts, APIs, infrastructure, or other participants.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported through the designated security channel. Sellers should maintain reasonable security practices and disclose material incidents/vulnerabilities affecting an offer when required.

## 23. Privacy and Data Minimization
TechFoundX should collect/process only information reasonably necessary for marketplace operation, verification, security, transactions, support, legal compliance, and legitimate business purposes.

Data classes:
- Public
- Account-only
- Verification-only
- Seller-confidential
- Transaction-confidential
- Restricted internal/security information

A separate Privacy Policy and, where applicable, Data Processing Addendum should govern detailed processing, retention, rights, transfers, and cookies.

## 24. Data Retention and Audit Records
Records may be retained as necessary for security, disputes, legal compliance, accounting, transaction history, verification, and marketplace integrity, subject to law and the retention schedule.

Sensitive evidence must have restricted access and must not be exposed in public listings.

## 25. Suspension and Termination
TechFoundX may restrict, delist, suspend, or terminate access for material breach, fraud, security abuse, false verification, IP/legal concerns, unlawful conduct, marketplace manipulation, repeated violations, failure to provide evidence, or legal requirements.

Immediate action may be taken where delay creates material risk.

## 26. Appeals
Eligible enforcement decisions may be appealed through the designated process. Appeals should identify the decision, facts, evidence, and requested correction. Where practical, an independent reviewer should assess the appeal.

## 27. Participant Disputes
Unless TechFoundX has expressly become a contracting party, disputes about ownership, performance, payment, delivery, support, or transaction obligations are primarily between the participants who entered the agreement.
